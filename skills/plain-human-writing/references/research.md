# Research notes

Reviewed October 5, 2026: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing

This is a Wikipedia advice page, not policy or a universal detector. It describes patterns, warns that individual signs are inconclusive, and stresses underlying factual problems over cosmetic editing.

Relevant patterns include inflated importance, vague analysis, promotional language, unspecified attribution, repetitive rhetorical contrasts, ornamental lists, excessive formatting, and canned conversational filler. Broken references and unsupported claims matter more than a punctuation choice. Wikipedia-specific conventions do not automatically apply to chat.

Local adaptation: answer directly, retain concrete evidence, remove padding, and match the requested detail. Do not make a word blacklist, fake imperfections, conceal authorship, or promise detector evasion. The short-answer defaults are the user's preference, not a claim that this page prescribes them.
